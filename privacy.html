Mira privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Mira is a personal AI assistant app operated by an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains what data Mira handles and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password hash (managed by Supabase Auth).
  • Content you give Mira: chat messages, tasks, ideas and lists, memory entries about your preferences, and a log of the actions Mira takes for you.
  • Device: a push-notification token so Mira can notify you.
  • Google data (only if you connect Google): your Google Calendar events and calendar list, read through Google's OAuth permission screen that you approve.

How we use it

Only to provide the app's features to you: answering your requests, planning your day, creating and moving time blocks that Mira makes on your calendar, and reminding you. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with data brokers.

Google user data

Mira's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is used only to show and plan your schedule and to create or change events that you have approved or that Mira created for you. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train generalized AI models. No human reads your Google data, except where you explicitly ask us for support and share it, or where required by law.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase stores your account and content (hosted in India, Mumbai region).
  • Anthropic (Claude) processes the text of your requests, and any calendar or task details needed to answer them, to generate Mira's replies.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • Google provides Calendar access when you connect it.

Retention, export and deletion

Your data stays until you delete it. In the app you can export all your data and delete your account, which permanently removes your account and all content stored with it. You can also disconnect Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops Mira's access to your calendar.

Security

Data is stored with row-level access controls so that each user can reach only their own records, and connections use encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong password.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: open an issue at github.com/jaishankarn/mira-legal/issues.
