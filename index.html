Mira

Mira is a personal AI assistant app. You chat with it, and it does the work for you: planning your day and week, keeping your tasks, ideas and wish lists, remembering your preferences, drafting messages and helping you reach your goals.

With your permission, Mira connects to your Google Calendar so it can read your events and add time blocks to your calendar for the plans you approve. You can disconnect at any time.

Mira is a personal project operated by an individual developer and is currently used by its developer and a small number of testers.
